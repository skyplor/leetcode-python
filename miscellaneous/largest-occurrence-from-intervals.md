Example: input [[1, 10], [1, 3], [11, 24], [2, 4]].
return 2 or 3 because 2 is coming in three intervals ([1, 10], [1, 3], [2, 4]).
Similarly 3 is coming in three intervals ([1, 10], [1, 3], [2, 4]).
